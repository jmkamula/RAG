# Ship 73'.b — 19 GDPR bridges authored

## What shipped

Task #595 opened 22 tenant-facing GDPR articles as unbridged. Ship
73'.a triaged them into 14 bridgeable + 7 legitimate carve-outs
+ 1 already-bridged, with 22 draft edge proposals. Ship 73'.b
refined the draft list and authored 19 defensible edges.

- **Drops (3)** for insufficient auditor-defensibility:
  - Art.41 → A.5.35 (independent review of infosec ≠ code-of-conduct
    monitoring body oversight — analogical, not implementational)
  - Art.42 → A.5.1 GOVERNANCE (certification claims reflect in policy
    but this isn't a strong governance bridge)
  - Art.43 → A.5.19 (certification bodies aren't quite suppliers)
- **Upgrades (2)**:
  - Art.42 → A.5.36 low → high (compliance with policies/standards
    IS what GDPR certification externally attests)
  - Art.87 → A.8.11 medium → high (national ID pseudonymisation is
    exactly A.8.11's purpose)

## Deliberate carve-outs (unchanged from 73'.a)

7 tenant-facing GDPR articles remain deliberately unbridged:
- **Art.77-80** — data subject remedies (right to lodge complaint,
  judicial remedy vs SA, judicial remedy vs controller, representation).
  These are data-subject rights of action, not organizational controls.
- **Art.81** — suspension of proceedings (procedural).
- **Art.84** — penalties (Member State law outcome).
- **Art.86** — public access to official documents (mostly N/A
  private orgs).
- **Art.91** — church-affiliated existing rules (narrow context, no
  general ISO mapping).

Leaving these unbridged is honest signal (Ship 68'.b principle):
the framework mapping catalog claims no attribution where none
exists.

## Codified lesson (small)

### 47. Draft proposals ≠ authored bridges

Ship 73'.a produced 22 draft edges with confidence signals attached.
Ship 73'.b's authoring stage refined that list — 3 drops for
auditor-defensibility + 2 upgrades — before writing anything into
the catalog. The confidence signal wasn't decorative; it was a
review checkpoint. Every arc that runs a draft → refine → author
pipeline should keep the review checkpoint honest, even under time
pressure.
