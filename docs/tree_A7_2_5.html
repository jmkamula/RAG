A.7.2.5
Privacy impact assessment
ISO 27701:2019
PII processing generates risks for PII principals which should be assessed through a privacy impact assessment. Some jurisdictions mandate PIAs in specific cases (e.g. automated decisions with legal effects, large-scale processing of special categories, systematic monitoring of public spaces). ISO/IEC 29134 provides guidance.
