# Working in Secure Areas Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> A.7.6 requires security measures for working in secure areas. The procedure documents secure-area definition, device restrictions, escort requirements, clean entry/exit. The work session register, applicable-areas scope and periodic review are sibling leaves

## What this template gives you

This template helps you set clear rules for working in secure areas, including who can enter, what devices are allowed, and how to keep records of work sessions. It's designed to help you meet ISO 27001 requirements for physical security.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever your organization has areas that need extra security controls, such as server rooms or restricted offices. Review and update it whenever your secure areas or security practices change.
