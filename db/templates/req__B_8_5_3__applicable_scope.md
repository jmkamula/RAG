# Applicable Processor Disclosure Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Every context where customer PII is disclosed to a third party by the processor.

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly outline when and how customer personal information is shared with third parties, making it easier to demonstrate compliance with privacy standards.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever your organization discloses customer personal data to another company on their behalf, and update it whenever there are changes to your data sharing practices.
