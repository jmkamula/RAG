## What this template gives you

This template helps you keep a clear record of how your organization reviews and manages disclosure requests, ensuring notifications are timely, legal reviews are consistent, and compliance with gag orders is well documented.

## When to use it

Use this template if your organization handles disclosure requests and needs to verify its processes annually, especially when your activities match certain privacy-related triggers.
