# DPO Tasks Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Art.39 enumerates the DPO's tasks (Art.39.1 a-e + Art.39.2 risk-based approach). The procedure documents how each task is operationalised in practice

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly document how your Data Protection Officer (DPO) carries out their key responsibilities under GDPR, making it easier to show compliance during audits or reviews.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever your organization is required to appoint a DPO, or when your data processing activities match the relevant GDPR criteria. Update it whenever there are changes to your DPO's tasks or processes.
