# Operational Planning and Control Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Clause 8.1 requires the organisation to plan, implement, and control processes to meet ISMS requirements and to implement actions determined in Clause 6. The procedure is the canonical artefact. Sibling leaves: operational execution register, applicable processes scope, program review

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how your organization plans, implements, and controls its key information security processes, ensuring you meet ISO 27001 requirements and can demonstrate a clear approach to operational planning.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever you need to describe how your organization manages and controls its information security processes. Review and update it whenever your processes change or as part of your regular ISMS maintenance.
