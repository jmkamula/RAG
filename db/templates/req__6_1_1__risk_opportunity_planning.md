# ISMS Risk and Opportunity Planning Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Clause 6.1.1 requires the organisation to consider 4.1 issues + 4.2 requirements and determine risks and opportunities. The procedure is the canonical artefact linking inputs to actions. Sibling leaves: action register, applicable inputs scope, program review

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how your organization identifies and manages risks and opportunities related to your information security management system. It connects your business context and stakeholder needs to practical actions.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever you need to show how your organization considers internal and external issues, as well as stakeholder requirements, to address risks and opportunities. Review and update it whenever significant changes occur.
