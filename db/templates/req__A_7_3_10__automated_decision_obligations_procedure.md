# Automated Decision Subject Obligations Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §7.3.10 requires the org to identify + address obligations to subjects arising from solely-automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects. Companion to the already-curated GDPR Art.22 4-leaf (this is the 27701-side counterpart).

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how your organization handles obligations to individuals affected by decisions made entirely by automated systems, especially when those decisions have significant legal or similar impacts.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever your organization makes important decisions about people using only automated processes, and update it whenever your practices or systems change in ways that affect these obligations.
