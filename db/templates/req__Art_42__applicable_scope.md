## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly define which certification schemes and processing activities are relevant to your organization, and explains the business reasons for seeking certification under GDPR.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever your organization is considering GDPR certification or needs to clarify the scope of certification, especially if your business profile or processing activities change.
