# Right to Object Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Art.21 gives subjects the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests / public task (Art.21.1) AND an absolute right to object to direct marketing (Art.21.2). Distinct workflows for absolute (direct marketing) vs balancing (legitimate interests)

## What this template gives you

This template helps you create a clear procedure for handling individuals’ objections to your use of their personal data, including both direct marketing and other legitimate interests under GDPR.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you process personal data and need to address requests from people who object to your data use, especially for direct marketing or when balancing your interests against their rights. Update the procedure whenever your practices or legal requirements change.
