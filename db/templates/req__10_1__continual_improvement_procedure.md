# Continual Improvement Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Clause 10.1 requires the organisation to continually improve the suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the ISMS. The procedure is the canonical artefact with triggers, targets, and tracking. Sibling leaves: improvement action register, applicable triggers scope, program review

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how your organization will keep improving your information security management system, making sure it stays effective and up to date.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you need to show that your improvement process is active and ongoing, and update it as needed to reflect changes or new improvement actions.
