# Reporting Audience Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream that drives the procedure's audience and the awareness-promotion focus. Documents who CAN report (every person with access — including non-employees), who SHOULD know about the channel, and how the channel surfaces to each segment

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly define who can report issues, who should be aware of your reporting channels, and how those channels are made visible to different groups, including non-employees.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever you need to outline or update the scope of your reporting audience, especially when there are changes in access or reporting procedures. Review and refresh it as needed to keep it accurate.
