# PII Copy Provision Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §7.3.8 requires the org to provide a copy of processed PII on request. Bridges to Art.15.3 (copy of PII) + Art.20 (portability — structured, commonly used, machine-readable format).

## What this template gives you

This template helps you create a clear procedure for providing individuals with a copy of their personal data upon request, ensuring compliance with privacy regulations and supporting data portability requirements.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever someone requests a copy of their personal information your organization processes. Update the document as needed, especially if your data handling practices or legal requirements change.
