# Top Management Approval of Segregation Matrix

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Clause 5.3 makes assignment of conflicting-duty controls a top-management responsibility. The approval may be a signed cover page on the matrix, a board minute, or a delegated equivalent — any form that names a top-management signatory, a date, and the specific matrix version being approved

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document top management’s formal approval of your segregation of duties matrix, ensuring clear accountability and compliance with ISO 27001 requirements.

## When to use it

Use this whenever your organization needs to show that top management has reviewed and approved the latest version of your segregation matrix, and update it whenever there are changes to the matrix or its signatories.
