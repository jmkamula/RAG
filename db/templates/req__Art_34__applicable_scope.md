# Applicable Art.34 Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream — operational interpretation of 'high risk to rights and freedoms', boundary with Art.33 (lower threshold + different audience), Art.34.3 exception criteria documented

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly define when a data breach is considered high risk under GDPR Article 34, and explains the difference from Article 33 and any exceptions that may apply.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever you need to clarify the scope of Article 34 obligations in your environment, and update it whenever there are changes to your data processing or risk assessments.
