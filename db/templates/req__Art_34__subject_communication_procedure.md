# Art.34 Subject Communication Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Art.34 requires the controller to communicate the breach to affected data subjects 'without undue delay' when the breach is likely to result in a high risk to their rights and freedoms (with Art.34.3 exceptions). Companion to Art.33 procedure but with different threshold + audience + exceptions

## What this template gives you

This template helps you create a clear process for notifying people if their personal data has been compromised in a way that could seriously affect their rights or freedoms, as required by GDPR Article 34.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever a data breach occurs that could pose a high risk to individuals, and update it whenever your processes or legal requirements change.
