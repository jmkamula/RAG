# SIG Engagement Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> A.5.6 expects active engagement, not nominal membership. The procedure documents how SIGs are joined, how value is captured back into the organisation (intel sharing into threat-intelligence A.5.7, runbook updates, training inputs) and how dormant memberships are pruned

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how your team actively participates in Special Interest Groups (SIGs), shares valuable insights back into your organization, and manages SIG memberships effectively.

## When to use it

Use this whenever your organization is involved in SIGs, and update it whenever your engagement processes or group memberships change.
