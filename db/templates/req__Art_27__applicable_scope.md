# Applicable Art.27 Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream — whether the controller/processor is in scope for Art.27 (not established in EU AND processing falls under Art.3.2) and any Art.27.2 exceptions (occasional + no large-scale special-category + low risk + public authority)

## What this template gives you

This template helps you determine if your organization needs to appoint an EU representative under GDPR Article 27, and whether any exceptions apply to your situation.

## When to use it

Use this document when your business is not established in the EU but processes personal data of people in the EU, and review it whenever your processing activities or business profile change.
