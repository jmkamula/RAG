# Stakeholder Identification Framework

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The methodology that produces and refreshes the parties register — which categories are surveyed, how requirements are elicited (contract reading, regulator notices, surveys), on what cadence

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly identify and document all relevant stakeholders for your organization, ensuring you have a reliable and up-to-date register of interested parties as required by ISO 27001.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you need to establish or update your list of stakeholders, such as during initial setup or whenever there are changes in your environment or requirements.
