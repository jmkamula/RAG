# Art.36 Prior Consultation Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Art.36 requires the controller to consult the SA before processing where a DPIA indicates residual high risk despite mitigations. The procedure governs trigger detection, SA engagement, and the 8-week waiting period

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document the steps for consulting with the supervisory authority when a data protection impact assessment shows high risks that can't be fully addressed. It's designed to guide you through the required prior consultation process under GDPR.

## When to use it

Use this template whenever your data processing activities trigger a high-risk finding in a DPIA that remains even after mitigation, and you need to consult the supervisory authority. Update the document as needed if your processes or risks change.
