# Applicable Collection Points Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream — every collection point where personal data is taken directly from the subject (web forms, sign-up flows, in-app prompts, in-person, phone, email). Each needs the notice presented at the moment of collection

## What this template gives you

This template helps you identify and document all the places where you collect personal data directly from individuals, ensuring you present the required privacy notice at each point.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you collect personal data from people—whether online, in person, or by phone or email—and update it whenever your collection methods change.
