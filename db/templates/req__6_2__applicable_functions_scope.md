# Applicable Functions and Levels Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream that bounds the register — which functions and which levels are required to carry objectives (per clause 6.2 'at relevant functions and levels'); any explicit exclusions

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly define which parts of your organization are responsible for meeting specific objectives, and highlights any areas that are not included in your compliance program.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever you need to clarify which functions and levels are covered by your compliance objectives, and update it whenever there are changes to your organizational structure or responsibilities.
