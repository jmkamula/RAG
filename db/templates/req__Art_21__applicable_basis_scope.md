# Applicable Processing Basis Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream — which Art.6 activities are subject to Art.21 objection right (Art.6.1.e public task + Art.6.1.f legitimate interests + direct-marketing activities under any basis)

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly define which data processing activities in your organization are subject to individuals’ rights to object under GDPR, especially for public tasks, legitimate interests, and direct marketing.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you need to document the scope of processing activities that fall under the GDPR objection right, and update it whenever your processing activities or legal bases change.
