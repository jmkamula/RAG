## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly identify which approved codes of conduct apply to your organization’s sector or processing activities under GDPR Article 40. It ensures you’re referencing the right codes for your compliance needs.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever your organization’s profile matches specific triggers, such as changes in sector or processing activities. Update it as needed to stay aligned with any changes in your operations or applicable codes.
