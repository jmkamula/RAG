## What this template gives you

This template helps you keep track of your ongoing commitments under GDPR Article 40, making it easier to confirm that your code adherence programs are up to date and properly monitored.

## When to use it

Use this review record if your activities match certain GDPR triggers and you need to confirm compliance about once a year, ensuring all relevant obligations and monitoring engagements are still current.
