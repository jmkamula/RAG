# Applicable Member State Restrictions Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream — which Member State laws actually restrict Art.12-22/34 rights for the org's processing. Most orgs face none; regulated industries face several

## What this template gives you

This template helps you identify which specific Member State laws may limit certain GDPR rights in your data processing activities. It's especially useful if you operate in a regulated industry or handle data across multiple EU countries.

## When to use it

Use this document when your organization’s activities or industry profile suggest that local laws might restrict GDPR rights, and update it whenever your processing activities or relevant laws change.
