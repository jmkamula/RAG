# Top Management Approval of the InfoSec Directive

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Clause 5.1 leadership commitment requires top management to demonstrate active sponsorship of the ISMS — the directive's authority derives from that sponsorship. The approval may be a signed cover page on the directive, a board minute, or a delegated equivalent that names a top-management signatory, a date, and the specific directive version being approved

## What this template gives you

This template helps you formally document top management's approval of your Information Security Directive, ensuring clear leadership commitment and compliance with ISO 27001 requirements.

## When to use it

Use this template whenever you need to demonstrate that your organization's top management has reviewed and approved your InfoSec Directive, and update it whenever the directive changes or as needed.
