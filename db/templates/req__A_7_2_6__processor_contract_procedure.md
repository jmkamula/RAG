# PII Processor Contracting Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §7.2.6 requires a written contract with every PII processor, addressing implementation of Annex B controls (or their equivalent). Bridges to GDPR Art.28.3 mandatory contract terms (documented instructions / confidentiality / security / subprocessor consent / Art.28.3.h assistance / return-or-delete / audit rights).

## What this template gives you

This template helps you create a clear, compliant procedure for contracting with any company that processes personal data on your behalf, ensuring all key privacy and security requirements are addressed in your agreements.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you engage a new service provider or partner to handle personal data, or when reviewing existing contracts to ensure they meet current privacy standards. Update the procedure as needed when your processing activities or legal requirements change.
