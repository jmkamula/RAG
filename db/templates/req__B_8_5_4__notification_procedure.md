# Legally-Binding Disclosure Request Notification Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §8.5.4 requires the processor to notify the customer of legally-binding disclosure requests within agreed timeframes + agreed procedure. Handles gag-order exception.

## What this template gives you

This template helps you create a clear process for notifying customers when you receive a legally-binding request to disclose their data, including how to handle situations where you are not allowed to inform them.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever you receive a legal request for customer data that requires notification, and update it whenever your notification process or legal obligations change.
