# ISMS Resource Determination Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The methodology behind the resources record — how needs are estimated, what inputs drive sizing (risk treatment plan scale, objectives, training demand), who approves

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how your organization determines the people, skills, and resources needed to run your information security program, including how you estimate needs and who approves them.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you need to show how your organization decides what resources are required for your information security management system. Review and update it whenever your needs or processes change.
