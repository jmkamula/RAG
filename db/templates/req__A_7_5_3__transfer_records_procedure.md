# PII Transfer Recording Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §7.5.3 requires event-level recording of PII transfers to/from third parties + cooperation channels for subject-rights fulfilment. Includes modification / erasure transfers arising from subject requests.

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document every transfer of personal data to or from third parties, including how you handle requests from individuals to change or erase their information. It's designed to support your compliance with privacy regulations.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever your organization transfers personal data to or from third parties, especially when responding to requests from individuals about their data. Update the document whenever your processes or partners change.
