# Foreign Authority Disclosure Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Art.48 provides that any judgment or administrative decision of a third-country authority requiring disclosure of personal data may only be recognised if based on an international agreement (e.g. MLAT). The procedure governs how foreign-authority requests are received, evaluated, and responded to

## What this template gives you

This template helps you set up a clear process for handling requests from foreign authorities who want access to personal data, ensuring you comply with GDPR requirements and only respond when legally appropriate.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever your organization receives a request from a non-EU authority for personal data, and review or update it whenever your processes or legal obligations change.
