# Art.33 Breach Notification Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The procedure governing how Art.33 notifications get drafted, approved, and dispatched within 72 hours. The notification record is the per-event output; this is the operating discipline that makes 72h achievable

## What this template gives you

This template provides a clear, step-by-step procedure for drafting, approving, and sending GDPR Article 33 breach notifications within the required 72-hour window. It helps ensure your team can respond quickly and consistently to data breaches.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever a data breach occurs in your environment and you need to notify authorities under GDPR. Review and update the procedure as needed to keep it current with your practices.
