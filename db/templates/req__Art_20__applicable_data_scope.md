# Applicable Portable Data Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream — which Art.30 RoPA activities fall in Art.20 scope (basis consent or contract AND automated). Defines what 'provided by' the subject means operationally

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly define which personal data in your systems is subject to data portability rights under GDPR, including what 'provided by' the individual means in your operations.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you need to clarify which of your processing activities are covered by data portability obligations, and update it whenever your data practices or processing activities change.
