# Consent Management Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Art.7 requires the controller to demonstrate consent was given, in a clearly distinguishable form, with the right to withdraw being as easy as giving. The procedure is the canonical artefact — capture mechanism, withdrawal mechanism, evidence retention. Sibling leaves: consent register, applicable activities scope, program review

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how your organization collects, manages, and records consent from individuals, ensuring you meet GDPR requirements for clear consent and easy withdrawal.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever your organization collects or manages personal data based on consent, and update it whenever your consent processes change or new activities are added.
