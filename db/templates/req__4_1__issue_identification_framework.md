# Issue Identification Framework

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The methodology that produces and refreshes the issues register — what frameworks are used (SWOT, PESTLE, threat intel ingest), which sources are consulted, on what cadence. Without a documented framework the register is hard to defend as 'systematic'

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly document how you identify and update issues in your organization, including which frameworks and sources you use. It ensures your issues register is systematic and defensible.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you need to establish or update your process for identifying and recording issues in your environment. Refresh the document as needed to keep your methodology current.
