# Intellectual Property Rights Protection Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> A.5.32 requires appropriate procedures to protect IPR — both the organisation's own and third parties'. The procedure documents usage controls, third-party respect mechanisms, employee-creation rules and the linkage to acquisition. The licensed/IPR inventory, the acquired-works upstream and the annual audit are sibling leaves

## What this template gives you

This template helps you set up clear procedures to protect intellectual property rights, covering both your own assets and those belonging to others. It guides you through usage controls, respecting third-party rights, and managing employee-created works.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever your organization needs to ensure ongoing protection of intellectual property, as it should always be in place and updated whenever there are changes to your IP practices or assets.
