# Applicable Information-Society Services Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream — which services count as 'offered directly to a child' under Art.8. Critical for B2B / professional-service tenants who may incidentally process minors' data but don't 'offer to' them directly (e.g. a parent purchases on a family-account UI)

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clarify whether your services are considered 'offered directly to a child' under GDPR Article 8, especially if you provide B2B or professional services that might incidentally involve minors' data.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever your business activities could match scenarios where minors' data is processed, such as when a parent uses your service on behalf of a child. Update it as your service offerings or user profiles change.
