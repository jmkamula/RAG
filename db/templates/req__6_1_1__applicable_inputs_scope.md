# Applicable Planning Inputs Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream that bounds the procedure — which 4.1 issue categories and 4.2 party categories feed planning, what cadence inputs are refreshed at, what 'significant change' triggers ad-hoc replanning

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly define which types of issues and parties are included in your planning process, how often you review these inputs, and what counts as a significant change that requires an update.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever you need to outline the boundaries of your planning process, especially when your environment changes or at any time you review your planning approach.
