# Privacy Impact Assessment Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §7.2.5 requires a documented PIA/DPIA process — when to trigger a PIA, what the assessment covers, who signs off, when to consult the supervisory authority. Bridges to GDPR Art.35 (mandatory-DPIA triggers) + Art.36 (prior consultation).

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document your process for carrying out Privacy Impact Assessments, including when to start one, what to include, who approves it, and when to consult authorities. It's designed to help you meet privacy requirements under ISO 27701 and GDPR.

## When to use it

Use this template whenever your activities meet specific privacy risk triggers that require a formal assessment, such as processing sensitive data or large-scale profiling. Update it as needed whenever your processes or risks change.
