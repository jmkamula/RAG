# Subcontractor Engagement Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §8.5.7 requires customer authorisation prior to subcontracting + written contract with subcontractor implementing Annex B controls (flow-down).

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document your process for getting customer approval before hiring subcontractors and ensures your contracts with them include all required privacy controls.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you plan to engage a subcontractor and need to show that you have customer authorisation and proper privacy terms in place. Update the document as needed when your subcontracting practices change.
