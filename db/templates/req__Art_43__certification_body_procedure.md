# Certification Body Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Art.43 governs the accredited body that issues GDPR certifications. Applies when the org IS the certification body. Extremely rare for typical controllers/processors

## What this template gives you

This template helps organizations that act as accredited GDPR certification bodies document their procedures in line with Article 43 requirements. It's specifically designed for entities responsible for issuing GDPR certifications.

## When to use it

Use this template if your organization is an accredited certification body under GDPR, which is uncommon for most businesses. Update the document whenever your role or procedures as a certification body change.
