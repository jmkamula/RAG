# Applicable Legally-Binding Request Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Every legally-binding disclosure request (subpoenas, court orders, regulator inquiries with power to compel, national security letters).

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly define the scope of legally-binding requests, such as subpoenas or court orders, that your organization may receive. It ensures you document which types of requests are covered and how they are handled.

## When to use it

Use this template whenever your organization receives a legally-binding disclosure request that matches specific criteria, such as a subpoena or regulator inquiry. Update the document as needed when new types of requests arise or your processes change.
