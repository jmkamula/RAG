# Top Management Approval of Roles and Responsibilities Allocation

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Clause 5.3 makes the assignment of information-security roles and authorities a top-management responsibility. The approval can live inside the responsibility matrix as a signed cover page, in a board minute, or as a separate signed delegation — any form that names a top-management signatory, a date, and the specific allocation being approved

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document and approve the assignment of information-security roles and responsibilities by top management, ensuring clear accountability and compliance with ISO 27001 requirements.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever you need to formally assign or update information-security roles and authorities in your organization. Refresh it as needed, such as when roles change or during periodic reviews.
