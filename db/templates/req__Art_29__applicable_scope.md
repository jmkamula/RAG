# Applicable Art.29 Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream — which personnel categories act under controller/processor authority (employees + contractors + embedded vendor staff), distinct from sub-processors (Art.28 territory)

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly define which personnel, such as employees, contractors, and embedded vendor staff, are acting under your authority as a data controller or processor, separate from sub-processors.

## When to use it

Use this document when your organization needs to clarify the scope of personnel subject to GDPR Article 29 obligations, especially if your business profile or staffing arrangements change. Update it whenever there are relevant changes.
