# Applicable Automated Decision Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream — mirrors the Art.22 scope (solely-automated + legal or similarly significant effects). This scope note points at the Art.22 scope register to avoid duplication.

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly define which automated decisions in your organization fall under the scope of Article 22, ensuring you avoid duplicating information and stay compliant with privacy standards.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever your processes involve solely automated decisions that could have legal or similarly significant effects on individuals. Update it as needed, especially when your activities or decision-making processes change.
