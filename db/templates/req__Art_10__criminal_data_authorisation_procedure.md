# Criminal Convictions Data Authorisation Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Art.10 permits processing of criminal convictions / offences data ONLY under control of official authority OR when Member State law specifically authorises it with appropriate safeguards. The procedure is the canonical artefact. Sibling leaves: processing register, applicable legal-basis scope, program review

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how your organisation handles data about criminal convictions or offences, ensuring you meet GDPR requirements and have the right authorisations and safeguards in place.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever your activities involve processing criminal convictions or offences data, especially if your organisation is required to do so by law or official authority. Update it whenever your processing activities or legal obligations change.
