# Data Protection Impact Assessment Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Art.35 requires controllers to carry out a DPIA before processing 'likely to result in a high risk' (including Art.35.3 mandatory cases). The procedure governs DPIA triggers + drafting + DPO advice + Art.36 prior consultation pathway

## What this template gives you

This template guides you through the process of conducting a Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA), ensuring you meet GDPR requirements when handling data processing that could pose high risks to individuals’ privacy.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever your planned data processing activities might result in a high risk to people’s rights and freedoms, or when specific triggers under GDPR apply. Review and update as needed if your processing activities or risks change.
