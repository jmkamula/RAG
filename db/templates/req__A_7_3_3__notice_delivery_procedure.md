# Privacy Notice Delivery Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §7.3.3 governs HOW subjects actually receive + access the notice content determined in §7.3.2 — timely, concise, complete, transparent, intelligible, easily accessible + in clear plain language.

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how you deliver privacy notices to individuals, ensuring the information is clear, accessible, and meets regulatory expectations for transparency and plain language.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever your organization needs to provide privacy notices to people, especially when your activities match certain criteria. Update the document whenever there are changes in how notices are delivered.
