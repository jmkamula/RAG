# Processor Disclosure Recording Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §8.5.3 mirrors §7.5.4 for processor — records disclosures of PII to third parties with what/whom/when + source of authority.

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document every time personal information is shared with third parties, including details about what was shared, with whom, when, and under what authority. It's designed to support privacy compliance requirements for processors.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever your organization, acting as a processor, discloses personal data to an outside party. Update the record as needed whenever such disclosures occur.
