# Objection Handling Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §7.3.5 requires an objection mechanism. Two flavours: absolute (Art.21.2 direct marketing / Art.21.6 statistics) and balancing-test (Art.21.1 legitimate-interests processing). Companion procedure to A.7.2.2 lawful basis.

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how your organization handles objections from individuals regarding the use of their personal data, including direct marketing and legitimate interest scenarios. It ensures you meet privacy compliance requirements under ISO 27701.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever someone objects to your processing of their personal data, such as for marketing or statistical purposes. Review and update the document as needed, especially if your data processing activities or legal obligations change.
