# Nonconformity and Corrective Action Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Clause 10.2 requires the organisation to react to nonconformity, evaluate causes, implement corrective action, review effectiveness, and update the ISMS if needed. The procedure is the canonical artefact. Sibling leaves: NC register (per-NC lifecycle), applicable NC sources scope, program review

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how your organization identifies, investigates, and addresses problems or failures in your information security management system. It guides you through taking corrective action and keeping records for compliance with ISO 27001.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever a security issue or nonconformity is found in your environment, and update it as needed to reflect changes or improvements in your processes.
