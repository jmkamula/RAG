# Customer Subject-Rights Support Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §8.3.1 requires the processor to provide the customer (controller) with the means to comply with their subject-rights obligations. Covers technical support paths + informational disclosures + contract-specified support scope.

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how your organization supports customers in meeting their privacy rights obligations, including technical assistance, information sharing, and contract-specific support details.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever your organization is required to help customers comply with subject-rights requests, and update it whenever there are changes in your support processes or contractual commitments.
