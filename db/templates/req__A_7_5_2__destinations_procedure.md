# Transfer Destinations Documentation Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §7.5.2 requires the org to maintain + publish the list of countries + international orgs to which PII can possibly be transferred, including via subcontractors. Companion to §7.3.2 notice + §7.5.1 basis.

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document and share the list of countries and international organizations where personal data may be transferred, including through your subcontractors. It supports your compliance with privacy requirements related to international data transfers.

## When to use it

Use this template whenever your organization transfers personal data across borders or involves international subcontractors. Update the document as needed whenever your transfer destinations change or new ones are added.
