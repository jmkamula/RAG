# Customer Compliance Information Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> §8.2.5 requires the processor to provide the customer with information sufficient for the customer to demonstrate their own compliance. Governs audit-support pathway (Art.28.3.h), certification-sharing (SOC 2 / ISO 27001 / 27701), and ad-hoc information requests.

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document how you provide customers with the information they need to show their own compliance, including support for audits, sharing certifications, and responding to information requests.

## When to use it

Use this procedure whenever a customer asks for compliance information, such as during audits or when sharing certifications. Update it as needed whenever your processes or compliance status change.
