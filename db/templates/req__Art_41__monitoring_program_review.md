## What this template gives you

This template helps you keep track of your code monitoring program, making sure your accreditation is up to date and your monitoring activities are properly documented for GDPR compliance.

## When to use it

Use this review record once a year, or whenever your organization meets specific monitoring triggers, to confirm your program’s status and defensibility under GDPR Article 41.
