# Security Objective-Setting Procedure

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The methodology behind the register — how objectives are derived from policy + risk assessment + party requirements, who approves, what planning produces (resources, who, when, evaluation per clause 6.2 planning)

## What this template gives you

This template guides you through setting clear security objectives based on your policies, risk assessments, and stakeholder needs. It helps ensure your objectives are well-documented, approved, and aligned with ISO 27001 requirements.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you need to define or update your security objectives, especially when policies, risks, or requirements change. Review and refresh the procedure as needed to keep it current.
