# Privacy Notice (Data Collected Directly)

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> Art.13 requires controllers to provide a privacy notice when collecting personal data directly from the subject. The notice is the canonical artefact. Sibling leaves: publication record, applicable collection points scope, program review

## What this template gives you

This template helps you create a clear privacy notice for people whose personal data you collect directly, making sure you meet legal requirements and inform individuals about how their information is used.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you collect personal data directly from individuals, and update it whenever your data practices change or new types of information are collected.
