# Applicable Joint Controller Relationships Scope

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> The upstream — which multi-party PII relationships involve joint control (as opposed to controller-processor or independent controllers). Many orgs have zero joint arrangements.

## What this template gives you

This template helps you clearly identify and document any situations where your organization shares responsibility for personal data with another party, as opposed to acting alone or as a processor.

## When to use it

Use this document whenever you need to determine if you have any joint controller relationships, especially when your business activities or partnerships change. Update it as needed when new relationships are formed or existing ones change.
