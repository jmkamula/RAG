# Management Approval of the Acceptable Use Policy

<<DOC_CONTROL>>

> AUPs are enforceable only when management has explicitly approved them — the approval establishes the authority behind the disciplinary consequences. The approval names a signatory at the appropriate management level, a date, and the specific policy version

## What this template gives you

This template helps you document official management approval for your Acceptable Use Policy, ensuring it has the necessary authority and clarity on who approved it, when, and which version is in effect.

## When to use it

Use this whenever you introduce or update your Acceptable Use Policy, and refresh it whenever there are changes to the policy or management signatories.
